## How it works

### We define the role

First, I talk with the hiring manager. We clarify who you're looking for, what this person will work on, which skills matter, and what the interview should test.

Then I write an interview plan for the role: topics, criteria, questions, and scenarios. Every candidate goes through the same plan, so your team compares observations, not impressions.

Hiring several people for the same role? One plan covers them all. Different roles get separate plans.

### I interview the candidates

Before each interview, I read the candidate's CV and decide what to dig into within the agreed plan.

The interview is a structured technical conversation about experience, decisions, trade-offs, and situations close to real work. I also watch how the candidate asks clarifying questions and handles uncertainty.

It usually takes 60–90 minutes online. If the role needs it, we can add a separate coding stage.

### You get a written assessment

After each interview, I write a technical assessment: what the candidate demonstrated, which level is realistic for this role, their strengths, the risks, and whether it makes sense to continue.

It rests on the role requirements and what the conversation showed, not on “liked / didn't like” or chance.
