## Why technical interviews often fail

A bad technical interview looks fine until someone has to explain what the team learned about the candidate, and why they should or shouldn't move forward.

</section>

<section>

## How I conduct interviews

I don't fire random questions at candidates or check whether they guessed the answer I had in mind.

I want to see how someone thinks: how they talk about their experience, explain decisions, ask clarifying questions, and handle a task that isn't fully defined.

After the interview, your team gets a written assessment: what the candidate demonstrated, where the risks lie for this role, and why I recommend moving forward or stopping.

</section>
