## How I interview

<div class="section-body">

I don't fire random questions or check whether candidates guessed the answer I had in mind. I watch how they think: how they explain decisions, ask clarifying questions and handle a task that isn't fully defined. Every interview is recorded, so each judgement in the report rests on what the candidate actually said.
