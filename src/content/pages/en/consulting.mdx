## How it works

<ul class="bullets">
  <li>
    <span aria-hidden="true">1</span>
    <span>
      <strong>Define the role.</strong> A short call with you and, ideally, the
      client's hiring manager: what the person will do, which skills matter on
      day one, which can be learned later.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span aria-hidden="true">2</span>
    <span>
      <strong>Role profile and interview plan.</strong> Must-haves,
      nice-to-haves, red flags, and the topics and scenarios I'll use. You and
      the client approve it before the first interview.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span aria-hidden="true">3</span>
    <span>
      <strong>Interview.</strong> 60–90 minutes on real situations: decisions,
      trade-offs, debugging, and how the candidate handles an unclear task. You
      or the client schedule, host and record the call; I base the report on the
      recording.
    </span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span aria-hidden="true">4</span>
    <span>
      <strong>Report.</strong> A written assessment within 48 hours: a short
      version for the recruiter and a full one for the hiring manager.
    </span>
  </li>
</ul>

</section>

<section class="section-grid">

## What you receive

<div class="section-body">

Every report states whether the candidate passed the technical interview and at what level, judged only against the approved criteria. I don't give a hire or no-hire verdict; that decision is the client's.

<dl class="skills">

<div>
  <dt>Passed</dt>
  <dd> Meets every must-have. </dd>
</div>

<div>
  <dt>Borderline</dt>
  <dd>
    {" "}
    Partly meets one or more must-haves, with none missing; each gap is
    named.{" "}
  </dd>
</div>

<div>
  <dt>Not passed</dt>
  <dd> Doesn't meet one or more must-haves. </dd>
</div>

</dl>

Each report also gives the level (Trainee to Lead), a scorecard against the must-haves, and the strengths and concerns behind it. Candidate-facing feedback is available on request. Sample reports on request.

For roles outside frontend, the result and level cover the areas I assess; the stack check stays with the client.

</div>

</section>
