## Summary

Senior product engineer with 15+ years of frontend experience, focused on complex data and ML interfaces. I turn ambiguous requirements and legacy constraints into shipped product flows — discovery, architecture, implementation, release, and production debugging — and take on the backend and cross-stack work a feature needs, using AI assistance while keeping ownership of design, review, and verification myself.

Most recently at DataRobot, I worked on evaluation and legacy-platform migration for an ML product: shipped a customer-facing test-set evaluation feature, redesigned the upload validation flow for customer-defined evaluation metrics, and built a personal agentic workflow that let me run research, implementation, and testing in parallel across up to four workstreams. Earlier, I joined SkillReveal at inception as its frontend lead through its first paying customers, and led frontend delivery on real-time trading, visual editors, and analytics-dashboard products.

Looking for a Senior or Full-stack Product Engineer role, remote, at a product company building complex data or ML-driven interfaces.
