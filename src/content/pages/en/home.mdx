## Working principles

<div class="item-grid">
  <div>
    <h3>Research before scope</h3>
    <p> I study the domain, the legacy behavior and the code before I propose scope. </p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Small steps that ship</h3>
    <p> I cut work into the smallest end-to-end flows that are testable and useful on their own, and release them one at a time. No big-bang launches. </p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Check the whole flow</h3>
    <p> I trace the complete flow myself and confirm what I find with whoever owns the code. I don't take a colleague's assurance or an agent's report on trust. </p>
  </div>
</div>
